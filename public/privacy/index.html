Privacy policy · 5 September 2026

How Flipbook Proof handles your data

Flipbook Proof processes source video in your browser. The video and extracted frames are not uploaded to us.

No account is required. We do not use advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

Data stored on your device

Extracted frames and project settings use IndexedDB. This local storage lets a proof survive a refresh.

A Plus token and its last result use localStorage after you enter a token. Demo data uses separate keys and storage.

Use “Start over” to remove a project. Use “Remove license” to remove the token.

Data sent over the network

License verification sends the token over HTTPS to the Sociobot billing API. No video frame is part of that request.

Sociobot/Dodo processes checkout records. Flipbook Proof does not receive or store card details.

Your exports

A project export contains extracted frame images. Store the JSON file as carefully as the source footage.

Contact

Email privacy@sociobot.in with a privacy request.
